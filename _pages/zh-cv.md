- 使用技术：C++、CUDA、深度学习框架构建
- 代码规模：11,612 行 C++/CUDA 代码
- 链接：[https://github.com/yibotongxue/my-tensor](https://github.com/yibotongxue/my-tensor)

- 代码规模：5,068 行 Python 代码
- 链接：[https://github.com/yibotongxue/llm-evaluator](https://github.com/yibotongxue/llm-evaluator)

- 链接：[https://github.com/yibotongxue/unify-llm](https://github.com/yibotongxue/unify-llm)
